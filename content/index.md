I'm Pablo Pazos Parada, an engineer from O Grove, Spain, based around Santiago de Compostela. I work in the OCTO team at [Omnia](https://useomnia.com), where I built Omnio, the Omnia Agent, from scratch: every user gets their own AI agent in its own sandbox, with memory of their brand. This is my personal blog: a place for thoughts, notes, and the occasional project.

I'm here for the 0→1: building what didn't exist yesterday, finding edges nobody bothered to push, shipping things that move something forward even a little. Lately that means the unglamorous part of agents: the harness, the sandboxes, durable state, recovery when things break. It also means making AI-assisted coding something I can trust, with formal models, conformance suites and review loops. This garden is my notebook along the way.

I write about agents, retrieval, infrastructure, mindset, and whatever I'm trying to understand well enough to build with. When I build something worth showing, it gets its own page, like [[agrex|Agrex]].
