I'm Pablo Pazos Parada, an Applied AI Engineer from O Grove, Spain, currently based around Santiago de Compostela. This is my personal blog: a place for thoughts, notes, and the occasional project.

I'm here for the 0→1: building what didn't exist yesterday, finding edges nobody bothered to push, shipping things that move something forward even a little. AI agents, infra, automation, open source — if it wasn't moving and now it is, I want to be part of it. This garden is my notebook along the way.

I write about agents, retrieval, automation, vision, mindset, and whatever I'm trying to understand well enough to build with. When I build something worth showing, it gets its own page, like [[agrex|Agrex]].
