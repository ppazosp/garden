[OSIX Tech](https://osix.tech) is the AI company I co-founded in June 2025 with six classmates from Computer Engineering.

I'm the CTO, which means I lead engineering, scope directly with clients, design the architecture, build core AI systems, and keep production deployments alive after the demo. The work usually starts with a real operational bottleneck and ends as a system connected to the client's actual tools, data, and workflows.

## How I Work There

- Diagnose the process with the people who feel the bottleneck.
- Scope an AI system around the workflow, not around a model demo.
- Build the architecture, product surface, integrations, and agent logic.
- Ship pilots, iterate with users, and move the useful parts toward production.
